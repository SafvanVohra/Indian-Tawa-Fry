Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book a table in advance?

Walk-ins are always welcome, but on weekends and holidays we recommend booking so we can keep a table ready for you. Use our Book Table page or call us.

Can I order food for takeaway?

Yes. Browse the menu, add dishes to your order and send it to us on WhatsApp — or simply call and we'll have it packed when you arrive.

Do you take party and bulk orders?

We do — birthdays, family get-togethers and office parties. Send us the date, number of guests and the dishes you like using the form above, choosing “Party / Bulk Order” as the topic.

Do you serve vegetarian food?

Yes, we have a dedicated vegetarian section with paneer specials and sizzlers. Look for the green mark on the menu.
