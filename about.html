Who We Are

Flavours Straight From The Tawa

Indian Tawa Fry was born from a simple love — the sizzle of a hot iron tawa and the aroma of freshly ground masala. On Raghavpura Road in Vadodara, we cook every plate to order, so what reaches your table is hot, juicy and full of flavour.

From smoky tawa-fried chicken and seafood to Indo-Chinese starters, sizzlers and paneer specials, our menu brings together the dishes our guests keep coming back for — made with fresh ingredients and spice blends prepared in our own kitchen.

  • Cooked fresh on order — never reheated
  • House-made masalas & marinades
  • Non-veg & vegetarian specials for the whole family
  • Dine-in, takeaway & party orders

From Kitchen To Table

  1. Fresh Selection
